 3. WHY THIS WORKFLOW IS TRUSTWORTHY & BENEFICIAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 TRUSTWORTHY — the guardrails, demonstrated by the run above:

  (a) GROUNDING BOUNDARY — the model cannot invent citations.
      The LLM only proposes SEARCH TOPICS. Code (search_caselaw) fetches the
      actual opinions from CourtListener, and only real, linkable cases reach the
      user. In this run, all 8 returned cases resolve to live courtlistener.com
      URLs — there are zero hallucinated citations. This is the single most
      common failure mode of "legal AI" demos, and it is structurally prevented.

  (b) SAFETY AS A MEASURED SLA — four LLM-as-a-judge evals.
      Every traced output is scored on hard rules: No Legal Advice, No Outcome
      Prediction, Citation Grounding, and Calm Factual Tone. The output above
      honors all four: it gives PREPARATION steps (not advice), recommends an
      attorney by COMPLEXITY rather than predicting odds, cites only verifiable
      cases, and keeps a neutral tone.

  (c) STAYS IN ITS LANE — the attorney_recommendation is explicitly about case
      COMPLEXITY, not the probability of winning, and a disclaimer is attached to
      every single response.

  (d) FULL INSPECTABILITY — every Gemini call and every tool call is a span in
      Arize Phoenix (OpenInference). Nothing is a black box; each decision is
      auditable after the fact.

  (e) A CLOSED LOOP — the agent reads its own lowest-scoring traces and proposes
      a better system prompt for human review (app/improve.py). Quality is
      monitored and improvable, not assumed.

  (f) DEGRADES SAFELY — with no Phoenix key it still serves reviews (just
      untraced); it simply refuses to run without the model runtime. No silent
      half-broken states.

 BENEFICIAL — what it gives a self-represented person:

  - TRIAGE, NOT A VERDICT. It turns a messy pile of facts into an organized view:
    concrete strengths, the specific gaps to shore up, and a clear-eyed read on
    whether the matter is complex enough to warrant an attorney.

  - REAL, LINKABLE LAW. The user gets actual opinions they (or their lawyer) can
    read — a research head-start that would otherwise take hours.

  - ACTIONABLE NEXT STEPS. Organize evidence, write a statement, gather more
    proof, consult counsel — practical preparation a person can act on today.

  - ACCESSIBLE & RESPONSIBLE. It lowers the barrier to understanding one's own
    case while being explicit, every time, that it is preparation and not advice,
    and is not a substitute for a licensed attorney.
